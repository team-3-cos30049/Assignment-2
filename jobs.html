Marketing Generalist

Reference number: MKT01

"A generalist understands that tactics without strategy is the noise before defeat, but strategy without execution is just a daydream. Our job is to bridge the gap between business reality and creative magic."

— Mark Ritson, marketing professor, consultant, and columnist

About the role:

We are hiring a Marketing Generalist to own our day-to-day marketing execution and help shape our strategy, working with Sales and IT so that what we promote matches what we can deliver. You will report to the Marketing Manager, with a dotted line to Sales and the UX / Technical team.

What you will do:

  • Plan and run campaigns across email, content, social, and events against agreed targets
  • Turn product and technical information from IT into clear messaging for customers
  • Work with UX and engineering to turn new site features, redesigned product pages, and improved application workflows into clear customer-facing messaging
  • Use customer behavior data (conversion rates, drop-off points, search terms) to suggest improvements to product listings and key user journeys
  • Partner with Sales on lead quality, handoff, and feedback on what is converting
  • Run A/B tests with the UX team and report what the results mean for marketing
  • Track performance in Google Analytics and our CRM, and report monthly on conversion rate, customer acquisition cost, and repeat purchase rate
  • Check campaigns against relevant requirements before launch, including consumer protection, advertising standards, and data privacy
  • Support the response to public-facing problems (outages, listing errors, customer complaints) by following our escalation plan, owned by the Marketing Manager

What success looks like:

  • First 30 days: you understand our customers, platform, product catalog, and current channels
  • First 90 days: you own a specific campaign or channel and reach its agreed target
  • First 12 months: a concrete outcome such as a repeatable acquisition process or improved conversion on key product categories

What we are looking for:

  • A balance of creative skills (copy, campaigns) and analytical skills (analytics, testing, reporting)
  • Ability to explain technical and UX topics in plain language to customers and colleagues
  • Clear, organized communication when handling several projects across teams
  • Interest in how users move through a website, from discovering a product to completing an application or purchase
  • Respect for working with a diverse team and across departments

What we offer:

  • Salary: AUD 75,000 – 90,000 per year, paid monthly
  • Annual performance bonus of up to 5% for meeting agreed conversion targets
  • Health insurance, 20 days paid leave, flexible standard working hours, and an annual learning budget of AUD 1,500
  • Growth path toward Senior Marketing Specialist or Marketing Manager, based on performance and role scope

Security Engineer

Reference number: SEC02

About the role:

As our Security Engineer, you will design, implement, and maintain the security controls that protect our platform, networks, and customer data. You will work closely with engineering, UX, and IT so that new features ship securely and vulnerabilities are fixed quickly. You will report to the Head of Security, with a dotted line to Engineering, the UX / Technical team, and IT.

What you will do:

  • Design and maintain security controls for our systems and networks, including firewalls, network segmentation, and encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Review new features, product listing changes, and online application workflows with engineering and UX before release, and flag risks early
  • Run regular security assessments, including vulnerability scans, penetration tests, and code and configuration reviews, and track findings through to remediation
  • Monitor network and application traffic for suspicious activity using our SIEM, IDS/IPS, and WAF tooling, and tune alerts to reduce noise
  • Protect customer accounts and checkout flows against threats such as credential stuffing, account takeover, and bot abuse
  • Lead the technical response to security incidents following our incident response plan, then write up what happened and what will change
  • Write and maintain security policies and procedures, and help the team follow them in practice
  • Keep our controls aligned with relevant requirements, such as PCI DSS, privacy laws, and industry standards
  • On-call arrangements, if any, will be confirmed in writing before an offer is made

What success looks like:

  • First 30 days: you understand our architecture, data flows, and current security tooling, and have reviewed open vulnerabilities
  • First 90 days: you own vulnerability management and alert triage, and reduce the average time to remediate critical findings against the agreed target
  • First 12 months: a concrete outcome such as a repeatable security review built into the release process and a tested incident response plan

What we are looking for:

  • Years of experience in security engineering, or a bachelor's degree in computer science, information technology, or a related field combined with strong hands-on projects
  • Solid knowledge of network security, encryption, firewalls, and intrusion detection and prevention systems
  • Experience with vulnerability management and security incident response
  • Ability to explain risk in plain language to non-security colleagues and help them make good decisions
  • Clear, organized communication when handling several priorities under pressure
  • Nice to have: certifications such as CISSP, CISM, or CEH, and experience securing e-commerce or web applications

What we offer:

  • Salary: AUD 110,000 – 140,000 per year, paid monthly
  • On-call allowance of AUD 500 per rostered week, plus time off in lieu after major incidents
  • Health insurance, 20 days paid leave, standard working hours, an annual certification budget of AUD 3,000, and a company laptop
  • Growth path toward Senior Security Engineer or Security Lead, based on performance and role scope

Sales Specialist

Reference number: SAL03

About the role:

As our Sales Specialist, you will drive revenue growth by finding and winning new business, building lasting relationships with clients, and explaining clearly how our platform helps them sell. You will work closely with Marketing and the Technical/UX team so that what you promise clients matches what the platform delivers. You will report to the Sales Manager, with a dotted line to Marketing and the UX / Technical team.

What you will do:

  • Find and qualify new opportunities through inbound leads from Marketing, outreach, referrals, and events
  • Run the sales process from first contact to signed deal, and keep records in our CRM
  • Present the platform's value to clients, including product listing tools, storefront features, and application and onboarding workflows
  • Build and maintain relationships with existing clients, and spot opportunities to expand their use of the platform
  • Analyze market trends and client needs to adjust your approach and suggest sales strategies
  • Share client feedback with Marketing and the Technical/UX team, so that common requests and onboarding problems reach the people who can fix them
  • Forecast your pipeline and report monthly on new accounts, revenue, conversion rate, and pipeline value

What success looks like:

  • First 30 days: you know our platform, pricing, and ideal client profile, and have shadowed client calls and demos
  • First 90 days: you are running your own pipeline and have reached the agreed number of qualified opportunities
  • First 12 months: you are meeting your agreed annual quota and own a client segment or territory

What we are looking for:

  • Years in sales, account management, or a similar client-facing role (e-commerce or SaaS experience is a plus)
  • Strong foundation in sales techniques, including prospecting, negotiation, and closing
  • Ability to explain a technical product and its value in plain language
  • Comfort with data: reading market and pipeline numbers and acting on them
  • Clear, organized communication when managing several clients and deadlines
  • Respect for working across teams and with clients from diverse backgrounds

Compensation and benefits:

  • Base salary: AUD 70,000 – 85,000 per year, paid monthly
  • Commission of 5% on new revenue each quarter, uncapped, paid with the following quarter's first pay
  • Quarterly bonus of AUD 2,000 for exceeding 120% of quota
  • Health insurance, 20 days paid leave, standard working hours, a phone allowance of AUD 50 per month, and sales coaching
  • Growth path toward Senior Sales Specialist, Account Manager, or Sales Team Lead, based on performance and role scope
